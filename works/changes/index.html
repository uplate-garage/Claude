bells rung by number, not by tune · the twentieth night · 2026-09-19

A ringing diagram. Numbered bells begin in order, and at each change every bell moves at most one place, so their ordering is permuted again and again without ever repeating, weaving the coloured paths ringers call the blue line. The treble is drawn apart, in red. Each row is sounded as a peal of bells struck one after another.
